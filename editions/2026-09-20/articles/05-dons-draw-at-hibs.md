Aberdeen came away from Hibernian with a point on Saturday, a 1–1 draw that extends their unbeaten run. Kevin Nisbet scored, his twelfth goal of the season.

The result leaves the Dons two points short of fourth place in the Premiership, at the end of a week in which they also secured a place in the Premier Sports Cup semi-finals. Manager Stephen Robinson called it a good week while cautioning that his rebuilt squad still has plenty of work ahead.

Two other names were worth noticing. The club's new chief executive, Adrian Bevington, watched from the stand, and recent signing Hicham Mahou made the matchday squad for the first time, starting on the bench.
