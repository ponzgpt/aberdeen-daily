Aberdeen drew 1–1 with Hibernian at Easter Road on Saturday, with Kevin Nisbet scoring his twelfth goal of the season. The result keeps the unbeaten run going as the Dons head into the international break, at the end of a week that also brought a place in the Premier Sports Cup semi-finals. Manager Stephen Robinson called it a great week for the club.
