Scottish Labour has a new leader, and he sits for this part of the country. Michael Marra, a Member of the Scottish Parliament for the North East region, was announced on Saturday as the successor to Anas Sarwar.

The BBC's profile describes his climb to the top of the party as a rapid one. For readers here the practical point is simpler: the leader of one of Holyrood's main parties now answers to North East voters at the ballot box.

What that means for local priorities is not yet on the record. This paper will report his first positions on issues that land in Aberdeen when he sets them out, rather than guess at them now.
