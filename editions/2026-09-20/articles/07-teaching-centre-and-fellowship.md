The University of Aberdeen and NHS Grampian have opened a General Practice and Mental Health Teaching Centre in the city, which the university describes as the first of its kind in the UK.

In the same week, a University of Aberdeen chemist was awarded a Royal Society University Research Fellowship worth £1.8 million. The university describes the fellowships as recognising potential leaders of the future.
