Negotiations over job cuts at a university covered by the BBC's North East service have broken down, after a union official was dismissed.

The dispute concerns a savings plan worth £10m, agreed earlier this year, which could mean the loss of more than 100 posts. The breakdown returns that plan to open conflict between staff representatives and management.

The report available tonight does not set out what happens next in the talks. This paper will follow it and name the steps as they are confirmed.
