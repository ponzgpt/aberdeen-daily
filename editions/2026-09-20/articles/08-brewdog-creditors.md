Administrators handling Brewdog after its takeover say the money available falls short of what creditors are owed, the BBC reports.

Among the debts they listed are about £489,000 in unpaid staff wages and holiday pay, and £2.4m in VAT owed to HMRC. The report does not say how much of those sums, if any, will eventually be recovered. This paper will report any update on what former staff are paid.
