Nadara, the developer behind the Bellrock floating offshore wind farm, has brought in XOCEAN to carry out survey campaigns along the project's cable corridor, Energy Voice reports.

Cable routes are among the first things a wind project has to settle on the seabed: the survey work establishes where export cables can run before anything is laid. For a city whose economy is tied to the North Sea, it is one more sign of where offshore work is heading.
