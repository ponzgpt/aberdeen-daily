If music is more your plan than comedy, the Tivoli Theatre has a tribute show almost every night from Wednesday. The UK Pink Floyd Experience plays on Wednesday, Heaven Can Wait brings the songs of Meat Loaf on Thursday, Hounds of Love celebrates Kate Bush on Friday and Thank ABBA for the Music follows on Saturday. The first two are listed with limited availability.

At His Majesty's Theatre, Scottish Ballet brings Emergence on Friday and Saturday, with a talk about the ballet on Friday for anyone who likes to know what they are about to see.
