The Aberdeen Daily

What's good in the Granite City today.

A free morning paper about what is good in Aberdeen: what's on, new places to eat, concerts and shows, and good news from the city. Assembled overnight from public sources and live data, and published only if it passes its own checks.

How it is written

  • It is positive and practical on purpose: crime, courts, accidents and scandal are another paper's job.
  • Every story is original writing about something first published elsewhere, and every one names and links its source.
  • The forecast is computed by code from live data, never typed by hand.
  • Nothing is invented: no venue, date, price or name that is not in the source material.
  • A quiet night makes a short paper. It is never padded.
  • An AI agent drafts the edition; an automated check rejects it if it breaks the house rules.

Sponsors

Local businesses can sponsor an edition. A sponsor gets one clearly labelled message in the email and on the site. Sponsors never appear inside news stories and never influence coverage.

Independence

The Aberdeen Daily is an independent project by Javier Ponz, who lives in Aberdeen. It is not affiliated with any outlet it cites. It is built on the open-source hermes-paper-agent and vael-paper by vaelkeep (MIT). Source: github.com/ponzgpt/aberdeen-daily.
